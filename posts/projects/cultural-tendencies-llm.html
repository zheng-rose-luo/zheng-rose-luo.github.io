Cultural Tendencies in Large Language Models

Overview

Tested whether large language models shift their expressed cultural orientation across different prompt languages.

Methodology

  • Curated a bilingual dataset of hundreds of matched Chinese and English ChatGPT API responses
  • Scored response quality
  • Identified validated cultural orientation scales from existing human participant studies

Publication

Lu, J. G., Song, L. L., & Zhang, L. D. (2025). Cultural tendencies in generative AI. Nature Human Behaviour.
