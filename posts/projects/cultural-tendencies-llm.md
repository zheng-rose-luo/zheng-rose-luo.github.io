# Cultural Tendencies in Large Language Models

From December 2023 to February 2024, I contributed as a volunteer research assistant to a project examining whether large language models express different cultural orientations when prompted in different languages. I worked under the guidance of Professor Jackson G. Lu and a postdoctoral researcher.

## My contribution

I evaluated the quality of hundreds of model-generated responses and reviewed human-participant studies to identify published measures of cultural orientation. These tasks brought me into contact with both the interpretation of model outputs and the measurement choices behind comparisons between languages.

## What interested me

The experience strengthened my interest in connecting observable model behaviour with its underlying computation. A change in a model's response can be a useful starting observation, but explaining it requires further work on the task, the model and alternative interpretations.

This is one reason I remain interested in artificial networks as scientific systems alongside biological neural networks. Comparisons can generate questions about learning and representation while preserving the distinction between the systems being studied.

## Related publication

The broader project was subsequently reported in:

Lu, J. G., Song, L. L., & Zhang, L. D. (2025). *Cultural tendencies in generative AI*. Nature Human Behaviour, 9, 2360–2369.
