Electric Fans, and Birds That Stay Young Till Death

Electric fans, and birds that stay young till death — both are things that bring happiness once you know them.

On a hot day, watching an electric fan gently send breeze in all directions — how can one not feel grateful for the many engineers and workers who designed and manufactured this wonderful device?

Learning that birds, for most of their adult lives, remain at the very peak of their vitality — how can one not imagine the biologists and science communicators who summarized and poetically phrased this conclusion?

There are so many things that bring happiness.

电风扇，和小鸟至死年轻

电风扇，和小鸟至死年轻，都是知道后令人幸福的事物啊。

热天看见电风扇和缓地向各处送风，怎能不感念设计制造这一妙物的众多工程师和工人师傅呢？

了解到鸟类在成年后大部分时间里都葆有生命最活力巅峰的状态，怎能不想象当时总结和诗意化这一结论的生物学家和科普博主们呢？

有许多令人感到幸福的事物啊。
