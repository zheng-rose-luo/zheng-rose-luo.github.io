# Art and Science Both Rest on Technology

---

Both art and science require technology as their foundation — like the stones of an endless staircase... no, like the central pillar of an infinite spiral staircase, guiding the way upward.

This thought came to me after watching an art video that demanded a high level of technical skill, and also from working on my own project, which calls for a great deal of technical know-how.

---

看见一个需要高技术力的艺术视频，加上自己的课题需要很多技术，有感。
