Artificial Creations, Artificial Creations Independent of Nature

Or perhaps — “Surely,” you say, “since humans are part of nature, their creations are natural too?”

No, no. Nature has limits; human artifice knows none. The achievements of the child should not all be credited to the mother and ancestors.

Actually, putting in some modern machinery wouldn't be out of place either — they are already magnificent enough. But what I really want to say is this: Mother Nature always acts according to fixed laws, whereas humans possess infinite imagination, creating things that did not exist before humanity — whether real or virtual.

I dare only to call them magnificent, not exquisite — and even "magnificent" feels like a stretch. But they are indeed ... special. Yes, they are.

On par with anthills, perhaps?

人工造物，独立于自然的，人工造物

什么？你是说人是自然的一部分，所以人工的亦是自然的吗？

不是的，不是的。自然有限度，人工无尽时。子代的功绩不应全部归功于母亲和先辈。

其实放些现在的人造机械也未必不可哈哈哈，它们已经十分宏伟了。但我其实只想说：自然母亲总是按规矩成律行事，人类却具有无限的想象力，制造出独立于人类存在以前的造物——无论是现实的，或者是虚拟的。

只敢说宏伟不敢说精巧，甚至宏伟也不敢说，但确实是……特别的。确实是的。
