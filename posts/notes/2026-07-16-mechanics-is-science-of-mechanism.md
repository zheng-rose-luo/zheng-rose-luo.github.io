# Mechanics Is the Science of Mechanism

---

**Mechanics is the science of mechanism.**

---

Hence, many subfields that are not traditionally associated with classical mechanics still need to be named with "mechanics" or "dynamics" in English, ending with *mechanics* or *dynamics*.

# 力学是机制学

---

**力学是机制学。mechanics is science of mechanism.**

---

故而许多与传统力学并不相关的细分领域，需要冠以某某（动）力学之名，英文则以 mechanics / dynamics 结尾。
