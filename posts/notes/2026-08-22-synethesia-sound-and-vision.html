Synesthesia of Sound and Sight — "See Through"

Crisp, crystalline vocal runs — like countless reflections of "you," each similar yet distinct, cast by a faceted glass.

This is synesthesia between sound and sight. The singer's highly articulated runs on the "glass ice white lace" passage — rapid pitch shifts across a single word — evoke the image of a faceted glass, refracting the same light, the same "you," into countless slightly different facets.

A textbook artistic presentation of synesthesia. Absolutely stunning.

Inspired by the "glass ice white lace" passage in See Through.

听觉与视觉的通感 — 《See Through》

极分明的转音，晶莹剔透如同被有棱角的透明玻璃杯所射出无数个近似却不同的，你。

这里是听觉与视觉的通感。歌手在 glass ice white lace 这一句上用了极具颗粒度的转音——同一个词在多个音高之间快速跳跃——那声音的棱角与折射感，让人仿佛看见一只带棱角的玻璃杯，把同一束光、同一个“你”，折射成无数个相似却不同的切面。

非常标准的通感的艺术化呈现。太惊艳了。

听《See Through》的glass ice white lace段有感。
