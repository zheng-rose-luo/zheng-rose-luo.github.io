AI, Giant Architecture, and the Vastness of Human Thought

Watching these AI-generated scenes of wind and clouds sweeping through colossal ancient buildings, I suddenly realized how immense the effort would be if humans had actually built this — and how effortlessly nature accomplishes it all.

Man-made clouds and mist require vast amounts of electricity. What do natural clouds and mist require? The sun! Ah, the eternal sun — and how small we humans are.

But how vast human thought is — vast enough to contain a cosmos far larger than the sun, vast enough to construct millions of universes greater than this one.

AI、巨构建筑与人类思想之宏大

看到这些AI画面里巨大的古建筑之间风起云涌，我突然意识到如果人力真的做成这个场景将要耗费多大，而自然完成这一切又如何不费力。

人造的云雾需要极大量的电力。自然的云雾需要什么呢？太阳！啊，永恒的太阳，而人类多么渺小。

但人类之思想多么宏大，足以装下远大于太阳的更大宇宙，足以构建比宇宙更大之千万个宇宙。
