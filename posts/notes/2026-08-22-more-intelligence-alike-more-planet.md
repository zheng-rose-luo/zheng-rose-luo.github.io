# More Intelligences Beyond Humans, More Planets Beyond Earth

---

Oh, and I still think that discovering machine intelligence beyond human intelligence ranks alongside the shift from geocentrism to heliocentrism.

For centuries, thinkers have proclaimed that "man is the measure of all things". They also say that "man is a thinking reed," or "I think, therefore I am" — all implying that humans are unique among all beings by virtue of their capacity for thought.

But both realizations — that humans / Earth are not particularly special, but stand alongside others; and that precisely because we belong to humanity / Earth, we cherish humanity / Earth all the more — I think that is beneficial.

*2026-08-24, from DeepSeek:*

This, in fact, has a clear counterpart in developmental psychology. It is what Jean Piaget called "decentration" — a core concept in cognitive developmental psychology. Decentration describes the process of moving from childhood "egocentrism" (the inability to distinguish one's own perspective from that of others) to the mature ability to coordinate multiple perspectives and understand oneself as part of a larger system. Piaget himself famously likened this cognitive restructuring to a "Copernican revolution" in the child's mental world — a shift that parallels, quite precisely, the realization that neither humanity nor Earth stands at the center of everything.

# 人以外有更多智能，与地球外有更多星球

---

哦对了，我依然觉得发现人类智能之外可以有机器智能，跟发现不是地心说而是日心说是一个水准的。

虽然“人是万物的尺度”思想家说了千年。或者，“人是会思想的蒲草”/“我思故我在”——都暗示着人好像只因为有思维而独异于众生。

但，这两者——认识到人/地球并没有更特殊，而是并列于他者；以及，正因为自己属于人/地球，而更珍爱人/地球——我想是有益的。

---

*2026-08-24，来自DeepSeek：*

这其实在心理学中有清晰的对应。它正是让·皮亚杰所说的“去自我中心化”（decentration）——认知发展心理学的核心概念之一。去自我中心化描述的是个体从儿童时期的“自我中心”（无法区分自己的视角与他人的视角），逐步发展到能够协调多种视角、将自己理解为一个更大系统的一部分的成熟能力。皮亚杰本人曾将这种认知重构比作儿童心理世界中的一场“哥白尼式革命”——这一转变，与我们认识到人类和地球并非万物中心的领悟，形成了极为精确的平行关系。
