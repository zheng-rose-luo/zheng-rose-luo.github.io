Solvent history strategy independently tunes stiffness and toughness in crosslinked
elastomers. Interesting results.

The data show a clean decoupling: the shear modulus shifts by almost an order of magnitude
while the fracture energy stays nearly constant. Preliminary theory suggests the ratio
$\nu_e / \nu_x$ is the hidden control parameter. Need to design a targeted experiment next.
